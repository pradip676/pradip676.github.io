My Story

I refused to wait until graduation.

I'm Pradip — studying Cybersecurity and Finance at the University of North Texas. Most students around me are focused on landing their first job after graduation. I'm more interested in building something before I even get there.

I started with small projects. Then bigger ones. Along the way I started posting the whole process on Instagram — not the polished version, but the real one. The confusion, the late nights, the small wins that actually matter.

My focus is the overlap between cybersecurity, finance, and tech — three fields colliding right now in ways that create real opportunity. I'm positioning myself at that intersection on purpose.
